# Retrieval and ranking

Production combines PostgreSQL English `websearch_to_tsquery`/`ts_rank_cd` with pgvector cosine similarity. Queries are parameterized. Explicit development exact mode computes cosine over stored vectors with a 1,000-candidate ceiling; it is a documented local alternative, not a keyword-only substitute or a production scalability claim. Tests use controlled vectors solely to test orchestration and boundaries, not model retrieval quality.

Both candidate streams apply access, subject, jurisdiction, issuer, approval, processing/lifecycle and applicability constraints before ranking. Shared/demo, private owner, explicit organization membership and company ownership are separate scopes. Demonstrations require request opt-in and remain labeled. Pending, rejected, failed, archived and inapplicable versions are excluded. Superseded versions can serve a matching historical period; publication date alone never selects legal applicability. Explicit historical searches require a known effective-from date and overlapping effective range.

Normalization expands CT, TP, DMTT, QFZP, FTA, IFRS, VAT and PE through an extensible dictionary. Reciprocal rank fusion combines lexical and semantic ranks, with a small contextual authority tie-break. Configured minimum semantic score, top-k and maximum context size constrain evidence. Neighbor chunks from the same eligible version preserve nearby conditions and exceptions. A hierarchy label distinguishes legislation/decisions, official guidance, standards, professional interpretations, internal notes and demonstrations; it does not automatically resolve conflicts or establish legal precedence.

Conflicting retrieved material remains available to the model with source type/date metadata and instructions to disclose conflict. Determining complex cross-document legal conflicts is not a separately verified reasoning engine. Official web verification is disabled. Empty retrieval means insufficient approved evidence, not proof of the absence of a rule. Retrieval coverage/quality must be evaluated against the real approved library and selected embedding model before release.
